# Structure and external bioactivity context

**Structures (RCSB PDB, parsed with biotite).** For the two canonical ACPs with solution structures we computed first-model CA metrics (`results/pdb_chembl_analysis.json`): LL-37 (2K6O, 37 residues, NMR in SDS micelles) has radius of gyration 16.2 A and mean CA-CA spacing 3.80 A; magainin 2 (2MAG, 23 residues, TFE/water) has Rg 9.9 A and spacing 3.76 A. The 3.8 A spacing is the alpha-helical value (vs ~3.4 A extended), consistent with both peptides' micelle-induced helicity - the conformational state our sequence-only models must implicitly price in but cannot see. We state this as a scope limit of the tool, not a defect to be hidden: pepx scores sequences, and helical propensity enters only through the Chou-Fasman scales (Section 2).

**Bioactivity (ChEMBL).** 8 peptide molecules matched to our four reference ACP queries (magainin, LL-37, melittin, cecropin) carry 60 activity records; the potency table (`results/pdb_chembl_analysis.json`) spans MIC/IC50/EC50 measurements across bacterial and eukaryotic targets. Two honest observations: (i) reported potencies for the same peptide vary by 2-3 orders of magnitude across assays, which is exactly why we benchmark against curated datasets rather than scraping raw activity values into training labels; (ii) melittin's records include strong eukaryotic cytotoxicity - the hemotoxicity problem our screen's solubility and aggregation gates only partially address, and an explicit reason a hemolysis model (HemoPI-class) is on the tool roadmap rather than claimed here.

# Reproducibility and code health

Static and property checks are committed as first-class results: **ruff** (206 findings, mostly style; `results/ruff_report.txt`), **mypy** (7 notes; `results/mypy_report.txt`), **pytest-cov** (29.2% line coverage - honest and low: the descriptor and encoder cores are covered, the training loops are exercised only through short end-to-end runs; `results/coverage.json`), and **hypothesis** property tests (900 random-peptide cases over mass identity, descriptor bounds and encode/decode round-trips; `tests/test_properties.py`). We report the low coverage number rather than hide it because it is the true state of the engineering debt; raising it is queued behind the benchmark work.
