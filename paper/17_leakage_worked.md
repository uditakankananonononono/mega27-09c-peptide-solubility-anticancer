# Train/test leakage audit (AntiCP 2.0 main)

The Appendix G note promised a homology audit; here it is. For every test peptide we computed the 4-mer Jaccard similarity to its nearest training neighbor (`results/leakage_audit_anticp2.json`): **zero exact duplicates**, mean nearest-neighbor similarity 0.373, but **16.6% of test peptides have a training neighbor above 0.7 Jaccard and 3.2% above 0.9**. Interpretation, stated flatly: the locked split is not duplicate-free in the homology sense, so part of every model's test score - ours and the published 0.83 alike - is nearest-neighbor memorization of near-homologs, not generalization. Two consequences. (i) The honest generalization number is lower than any reported test AUROC on this split; a homology-clustered split is the correct future benchmark, and our roadmap adopts it. (ii) The 0.83-vs-0.80 gap loses some of its meaning: if the published protocol's advantage came partly from split luck within the near-homolog stratum, neither number estimates what either model does on genuinely novel families. We keep the benchmark for comparability and say what it measures.

# Worked examples: four sequences end to end

**KLAKLAKLAKLAK** (designed ACP, positive control). Panel: MW 1395.84, charge +5.0, GRAVY +0.90, Boman 2.53, pI 10.6 (all computed; note GRAVY > 0 - the control would fail the candidate gravy gate, as a control should). TriNet: P_acp 0.990 / P_sol 0.639 / P_agg 0.462. Verdict: active as expected; the moderate solubility and aggregation scores are the known liability of the KLAKLAK amphipath - the tool says what the literature says, which is the point of a control.

**FEKEAKKIEIKRH** (v0.1 gated candidate). P_acp 0.903 / P_sol 0.867 / P_agg 0.055; charge +2.1, GRAVY -0.61, Boman 1.64. Honest detail: Boman sits near the low edge of its 1.5-3.2 band, not the middle - gates are ranges, and this candidate lives near one boundary, which the falsification protocol (Appendix F) is designed to stress first. Novelty: 0 hits in 9,683 sequences. This is what a clean screen hit looks like; the falsification path is Appendix F.

**IDKMFKKIGKKHE** (constrained-screen leader). P_acp 0.966 / P_sol 0.849 / P_agg 0.117. One methionine (oxidation-sensitive, unpriced by the gates - disclosed in Section 15.1) and a slightly higher aggregation score than FEKEAKKIEIKRH; ranked below it in our own judgment despite the higher activity score, which is why the paper names a family rather than a single hero sequence.

**DDKKFRKHCKWKEQKK** (constrained screen, instructive edge case). P_acp 0.940 / P_sol 0.836 / P_agg 0.057 with charge +5.1 and a strongly negative GRAVY (-2.17): the anionic N-terminus (DD-) plus cationic body is a surfactant-like asymmetry. It passes every gate and carries a cysteine (disulfide liability, unpriced - roadmap item 3). We include it as the clearest example of why gates are necessary but not sufficient: the screen nominates, the falsification protocol decides.
